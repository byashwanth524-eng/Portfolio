About Tappy Food

Founded in 2020, Tappy Food was born out of a simple passion for connecting food lovers with authentic local culinary experiences. We believe that good food has the power to bring people together, and we make it our mission to deliver that joy directly to your doorstep.

Our team works tirelessly with local chefs, cooks, and drivers to ensure that every meal is prepared with fresh ingredients and delivered with care. We pride ourselves on supporting local businesses, reducing our carbon footprint through eco-friendly delivery packaging, and maintaining high hygiene standards at all our partner kitchens.

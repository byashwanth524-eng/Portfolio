About Tappy Food

Founded in 2020, Tappy Food was born out of a simple passion for connecting food lovers with authentic local culinary experiences. We believe that good food has the power to bring people together, and we make it our mission to deliver that joy directly to your doorstep.

Our team works tirelessly with local chefs, cooks, and drivers to ensure that every meal is prepared with fresh ingredients and delivered with care. We pride ourselves on supporting local businesses, reducing our carbon footprint through eco-friendly delivery packaging, and maintaining high hygiene standards at all our partner kitchens.

Best Restaurants

Discover our top-rated local dining partners. Each venue is hand-selected based on food safety, ingredient freshness, user ratings, and culinary craftsmanship to ensure a memorable dining experience.

Bella Italia

An authentic Italian trattoria offering handcrafted pastas, wood-fired artisanal pizzas, and classic Mediterranean appetizers. All ingredients, from the rich San Marzano tomatoes to the extra virgin olive oil, are imported directly from Italy.

The Burger Shack

Famous for thick, juicy 100% grass-fed beef patties served on toasted brioche buns. The Burger Shack prides itself on fresh, never-frozen ingredients, hand-cut russet potato fries, and a secret house sauce that keeps patrons coming back.

Sakura Sushi

A contemporary Japanese dining destination specializing in sashimi, specialty rolls, and traditional bento boxes. Our master sushi chefs select the finest sustainably sourced fish daily from local markets to construct delicate flavors.

Sweet Treats Cafe

Indulge your sweet tooth with our freshly baked croissants, decadent chocolate lava cakes, gourmet cupcakes, and freshly brewed organic coffee. Our artisan pastry chefs use premium Belgian chocolate and organic sugar.
